## Create a post from a product

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a product">
    Open your store's **Products** tab, or a suggestion on **Overview**, and select **Post** on a product.

    <Screenshot name="store-product-post" alt="The Overview tab of a store with the button to create a post about a product marked" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the post">
    The post window opens with the product's name, price, description, photos and link already filled in. The text comes from your [product post template](/stores/product-post-templates).

    <Screenshot name="store-product-post-window" alt="The post window opened from a product, with its name, description, price, link and photo already filled in" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your channels and publish">
    Choose your channels and [publish or schedule](/posts/publish-and-schedule) as usual.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The product must be published or scheduled in your store first, because the post links to its page.

When you publish a product from OSCOHub, you're also offered the chance to create a post for it. If the product is scheduled, the post is scheduled for the same moment.

## About the photos

OSCOHub copies the product's photos and prepares them for social platforms, so they meet Instagram's format and shape rules. Photos with an unsuitable shape are placed on a white square rather than cropped.

These photos count toward your [upload limits](/plans/limits). If you've reached your limit, the post starts with its text only.

## What happens before the post goes out

A post written today might publish days later. By then the product may have changed, so OSCOHub reads it from your store once more just before publishing.

| What it finds | What happens |
|---|---|
| The product sold out, was unpublished or was deleted | The post doesn't go out, and you get a notification saying why. |
| The product is still scheduled in your store | The post waits for it, for up to a day. |
| The price changed | OSCOHub corrects the price in the caption where it can, and tells you. |
| The store is paused | The post fails. Resume the store, then publish the post again. |
| The store was disconnected | The post fails and can no longer be published. |

<Tip>
  The **Products** tab shows how many times each product has been posted, so you can filter for ones you've never promoted.
</Tip>

Whether your plan includes turning products into posts is shown under [Compare plans](/plans/compare-plans).
