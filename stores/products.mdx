Open your store from the sidebar. Its page has five tabs: **Overview**, **Products**, **Templates**, **Schedule** and **Settings**.

## Import your products

On the **Products** tab, choose how many to bring in, either the latest ones or **Everything**, then select **Import products**. A large catalogue can take a minute, and you can leave the page while it runs.

To bring in later changes, select **Sync now**. Each sync also refreshes the icon and logo OSCOHub finds on your store.

Importing in bulk [depends on your plan](/plans/compare-plans).

## See what to post next

The **Overview** tab suggests products that can be bought right now:

| Suggestion | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Bestsellers never promoted** | Products that already sell and have never had a post. |
| **New arrivals** | Added to your store in the last 30 days. |
| **On sale now** | Current discounts, ending soonest first. |
| **Top rated** | Products your customers reviewed best. |

**Fix before posting** lists products that would make a weak post, such as those with no photo or no short description.

## Find a product

On the **Products** tab, search by name or SKU. You can filter by category, stock, whether it's been promoted, and number of photos, then sort the results. A separate view lists your categories.

The **Schedule** tab shows your products on a calendar, on the day each was published or is scheduled to be.

## Add or edit a product

Select **New Product**, or open an existing product. Changes save **directly to your store**, and your store shows them as soon as you save.

| Section | What you can edit |
|---|---|
| **General** | Type, name, descriptions, prices, sale dates, categories and tags |
| **Photos** | Up to 10 photos. The first is the main image. You can reorder them, crop them and add alt text. |
| **Inventory** | SKU, stock quantity, low-stock alert and backorders |
| **Shipping** | Weight, dimensions and shipping class |
| **Linked products** | Upsells and cross-sells |
| **Advanced** | URL slug, catalog visibility, featured status, reviews and purchase note |

When you're ready, choose how to save: **Save draft** keeps it hidden from customers, **Publish** puts it on sale, and **Schedule** makes it go live at a time you pick.

Before changes go live on a published product, OSCOHub shows you what's changing so you can check it first.

To see the result, use **Live preview**, which is an approximation of a standard product page, or **On my store** to open the real page in your theme.

<Note>
  Some things stay in your WooCommerce admin. That includes variations on a variable product, and deleting a category.
</Note>

## Edit categories

In the categories view, select **New category** or edit an existing one. You can set its name, parent, description and image. Changes save directly to your store.
