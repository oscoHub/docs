OSCOHub connects to WooCommerce stores on WordPress. Other store platforms marked **Coming Soon** in the connect window can't be connected yet. Your plan sets [how many stores you can connect](/plans/compare-plans).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the connect window">
    In the sidebar, select **+** next to **Stores**, then choose **WooCommerce**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your store address">
    Enter the address customers use to visit your shop, such as `my-store.ma`, then select **Continue**. OSCOHub checks that it can find your store.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Choose one of the two ways below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="In one click">
    Select **Approve on my store**. We send you to your own store to approve the connection, so there's nothing to copy.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="With API keys">
    Select **Or connect with API keys instead**, then:

    1. In WordPress, open **WooCommerce**, **Settings**, **Advanced**, **REST API**.
    2. Select **Add key**, give it any description, and choose your admin user.
    3. Set **Permissions** to **Read/Write**, then select **Generate API key**.
    4. Copy the consumer key and consumer secret into OSCOHub, then select **Connect store**. WooCommerce shows them only once.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Your store now appears under **Stores** in the sidebar. Next, [import your products](/stores/products).

## Manage the connection

Open your store from the sidebar.

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Check Connection** | Tests that OSCOHub can reach your store and that its keys work. OSCOHub also checks every day. |
| **Pause Store** | Stops OSCOHub publishing to your store. You can still browse and sync products. Posts about its products [won't go out](/stores/post-about-a-product#what-happens-before-the-post-goes-out) while it's paused. |
| **Disconnect** (on the **Settings** tab) | Stops syncing and deletes the store's API keys. Your products stay in OSCOHub, and you can connect again at any time. Posts about its products can no longer be published. |

## Common problems

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="We did not receive the keys from your store">
    The one-click approval didn't finish. Try again, or use the API keys option.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="That store is already connected">
    You'll find it in the sidebar under **Stores**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="OSCOHub could not reach your store">
    Check that the site is online, then select **Check Connection** again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
