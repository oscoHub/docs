## File types and sizes

These limits apply to every file you upload.

| Type | Formats | Largest file |
|---|---|---|
| **Image** | JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP | 10 MB |
| **Video** | MP4, MOV, AVI, WebM, MKV, FLV, WMV, M4V | 512 MB |
| **Document** | PDF | 50 MB |

Your plan also sets [daily and monthly upload limits](/plans/compare-plans). A platform can still refuse a file OSCOHub accepts, so check its own rules if a large or unusual video fails.

## Facebook

| Type | Media | Text limit | Shape | First comment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Post** | Text, up to 10 images, or 1 video. No mixing. | 63,206 | 1:1, 4:5, 1.91:1 | Yes |
| **Reel** | 1 video | 5,000 | 9:16 | Yes |
| **Story** | 1 image or video. No text. | None | 9:16 | No |

## Instagram

Every Instagram post needs an image or video.

| Type | Media | Text limit | Shape | First comment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Post** | Up to 10 images or videos | 2,200 | 1:1, 4:5, 1.91:1 | Yes |
| **Reel** | 1 video | 2,200 | 9:16 | Yes |
| **Story** | 1 image or video. No text. | None | 9:16 | No |
| **Carousel** | 2 to 10 images or videos | 2,200 | 1:1, 4:5, 1.91:1 | Yes |

## LinkedIn

| Type | Media | Text limit | Shape | First comment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Post** | Text, up to 10 images, or 1 video. No mixing. | 3,000 | 1:1, 1.91:1, 4:5, 9:16 | Yes |
| **Document** | 1 PDF | 3,000 | Any | Yes |

## Threads

| Type | Media | Text limit | First comment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Post** | Text, up to 20 images, or 1 video. No mixing. | 500 | Yes |
| **Thread** | Several connected posts, each with up to 20 images or 1 video. No mixing. | 500 per post | No |

Threads files have their own size limit: images up to 8 MB.

## YouTube

| Type | Media | Description limit | Shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Video** | 1 video | 5,000 | 16:9, 1:1, 4:3 |
| **Short** | 1 vertical video under 60 seconds | 100 | 9:16 |

A title is required, up to 100 characters.

## Bluesky

| Type | Media | Text limit | Shape | First comment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Post** | Text, up to 4 images, or 1 video. No mixing. | 300 | 1:1, 16:9, 4:5 | Yes |
| **Thread** | Up to 25 connected posts, each with up to 4 images | 300 per post | Any | No |

Bluesky accepts images up to 1 MB, so OSCOHub tries to shrink larger ones for you. Videos can be up to 50 MB.

## Telegram

| Type | Media | Text limit |
|---|---|---|
| **Text** | None | 4,096 |
| **Photo** | 1 image | 1,024 |
| **Video** | 1 video | 1,024 |
| **Album** | 2 to 10 images or videos, which you can mix | 1,024 |
| **Document** | 1 PDF | 1,024 |

Telegram has no first comment.
