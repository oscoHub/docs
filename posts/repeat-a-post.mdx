## Set up a repeating post

<Steps>
  <Step title="Write the post">
    [Create your post](/posts/create-a-post) as usual and pick its first date and time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on repeating">
    Select **Post settings** at the bottom of the window, then turn on **Recurring post**.

    <Screenshot name="post-settings-recurring" alt="Post settings open in the post window, with Recurring post turned on and marked" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how often">
    Choose **Daily**, **Weekly** or **Monthly**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set an end date, if you want one">
    Use **Repeat until** to set the last day it can post. With no end date, it runs until you stop it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How the series runs

- Each time a post publishes, OSCOHub schedules the next one.
- The time of day stays the same in the post's time zone, including when the clocks change.
- A monthly post on a day the next month doesn't have moves to that month's last day, so 31 January is followed by 28 February.
- If a post goes out very late, OSCOHub moves on to the next date still ahead rather than sending a burst of catch-up posts.

## See and stop a series

Your series are on the **Recurring** tab of **Scheduling**.

<Screenshot name="manage-recurring" alt="The Recurring tab of the Scheduling page, showing a weekly post" />

To stop one, open the post's menu and select **Stop recurring**. No more posts are scheduled, and any already scheduled after this one are cancelled. The post itself is kept.

## Repeats and your plan

Each repeat counts as a new post against your [plan's limits](/plans/limits#how-posts-are-counted). If a repeat would go over a limit, or your plan no longer includes repeating posts, it's skipped and you get a notification. Check the series after you've upgraded or the limit has reset, in case you need to set it up again.

Whether your plan includes repeating posts is shown under [Compare plans](/plans/compare-plans).
