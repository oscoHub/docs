description: "Send a post now, choose a time for it, or save it as a draft, and see what happens when it comes due."
---

The button at the bottom of the post window does the publishing. Open the arrow beside it to switch between the three modes.

| Mode | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Schedule post** | Publishes at the date and time you pick. |
| **Publish now** | Publishes immediately. |
| **Save draft** | Keeps the post to finish later. It won't publish until you schedule it. |

## Pick a date and time

Select the date field, choose a day and time, then select **Apply schedule**. The time must be in the future.

### Time zones

Each post follows a time zone, shown next to the date. It starts as the channel's own [time zone](/channels/manage#change-the-time-zone). Check it if you're scheduling for an audience in another country.

## When a post comes due

Just before publishing, OSCOHub checks that the post can go out.

| What it finds | What happens |
|---|---|
| Everything is fine | The post publishes and moves to **Published**. |
| You sent one post to several channels | They go out over about two minutes rather than all in the same second, so platforms don't mistake them for spam. |
| The channel is paused or disconnected | The post fails and moves to **Issues**. |
| The channel's login stopped working | The post fails and moves to **Issues**. [Reconnect the channel](/channels/manage#reconnect-a-channel), then retry the post. |
| The platform's daily limit is reached | The post waits, and goes out once the platform allows it. See below. |
| Your plan has ended | The post is paused. See [when your plan ends](/plans/change-your-plan#when-your-plan-ends). |
| It's about a store product | OSCOHub [checks the product first](/stores/post-about-a-product#what-happens-before-the-post-goes-out). |

## Platform daily limits

Some platforms limit how many posts one account can publish through apps.

| Platform | Limit per account | Period |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram | 100 posts (a carousel counts as one) | Rolling 24 hours |
| Threads | 250 posts (a carousel counts as one) | Rolling 24 hours |
| LinkedIn | 150 posts | One day, in UTC |

A scheduled post that would go over stays pending, you get a notification once, and it publishes when room frees up. **Publish now** is refused instead, and tells you when it can go out.

OSCOHub counts only the posts it publishes for you. If you also post to the platform by other means, the platform may refuse a post before OSCOHub's count reaches the limit.

These limits come from the platforms. They're separate from your [OSCOHub plan's limits](/plans/limits).

## How you find out

Check the bell in the top bar. You get a notification when a post publishes or fails, along with the reason it failed.
