The image studio lets you crop and decorate an image without leaving OSCOHub. You can open it in two ways.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="For a post">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Add an image to your post">
        In the [post window](/posts/create-a-post), add an image.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open the studio">
        Hover over the image, select **Adjust aspect ratio**, then **Open Advanced Image Studio**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Save it">
        When you're done, apply your changes and name the design. The image goes back into your post, and a copy is saved to **My Designs**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="From My Designs">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Start a design">
        Select **Create**, then **Create Design**. You can also select **New design** on the **My Designs** page.

        <Screenshot name="library-designs" alt="The My Designs page showing saved designs, with the New design button marked" />
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose a base image">
        Upload a PNG, JPG or WebP image, give it a name, and select **Open Editor**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Save it">
        When you're done, apply your changes and confirm the name. The design appears in **My Designs**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## What the studio can do

| Tool | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Crop** | Set the shape, zoom and position. |
| **Collage** | Combine several images in a layout. |
| **Text** | Add text with your choice of font, color, shadow and outline. |
| **Filters** | Apply a ready-made look. |
| **Adjust** | Fine-tune brightness, contrast, saturation and more. |
| **Overlays** | Place an image or logo on top. |
| **Shapes** | Add shapes such as circles, stars and arrows. |
| **Stickers** | Add emoji-style stickers. |
| **Watermark** | Stamp your logo, once or repeated across the image. |

## Manage your designs

On **My Designs**, search by name, preview a design, or delete one from its **⋯** menu. Deleting a design can't be undone.

<Note>
  **My Designs** is a gallery, with no button to add a saved design to a post. If you want to edit an image for a post, open the studio from the post window, as shown above.
</Note>

Your plan sets [how many designs you can create](/plans/compare-plans).
