---
title: "Post templates"
description: "Save text you use often and drop it into any post."
---

A post template is reusable text, such as a sign-off or a promotion's standard wording. You can use the presets from OSCOHub or write your own.

<Note>
  Looking for captions filled in with a product's name and price? Those are [product post templates](/stores/product-post-templates), which belong to a store.
</Note>

## Create a template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a template">
    Select **Create**, then **New Template**. You can also select **New template** on the **Post Templates** page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill it in">
    Give it a name, choose an icon, and write the text you want to reuse.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it">
    Select **Save template**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use a template in a post

In the [post window](/posts/create-a-post), select the templates icon in the toolbar under the text box, then pick a template. Its text is added where your cursor is.

## Manage your templates

On **Post Templates**, filter by **All**, **Mine** or **Presets**, or search by name. Open the **⋯** menu on one of your templates to edit, duplicate or delete it.

To change a preset, duplicate it and edit your copy.
