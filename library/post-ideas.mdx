---
title: "Post ideas"
description: "Note your content ideas as they come, and track them until they're done."
---

Post ideas is a simple board for what you want to post about. An idea isn't a post, so it doesn't count against your plan's post limits and never publishes.

## Add an idea

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start an idea">
    Select **Create**, then **New Idea**. You can also select **New idea** on the **Post Ideas** page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill it in">
    Give it a title. You can also set a status, choose an icon and add details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save it">
    Select **Save idea**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Track your ideas

Each idea has a status: **Unassigned**, **To do**, **In progress** or **Done**. Change it on the idea's card as you go, and filter the board by status. You can also search your ideas.

Your plan sets [how many ideas you can save](/plans/compare-plans).
