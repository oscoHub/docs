<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the connection">
    In the sidebar, select **+** next to **Social media**, then choose **YouTube**.

    <Screenshot name="connect-card-youtube" alt="The Connect a channel window with the YouTube card marked" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in with Google">
    Log in with the Google account that owns the channel and accept every permission Google asks for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your channel">
    If your Google account has several channels, pick one on Google's screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To add another channel from the same Google account, repeat these steps and pick a different channel. It's added alongside your existing channels, not replaced.

## What you can publish

Videos and Shorts, each with a title and settings of its own. See [formats and limits](/posts/formats-and-limits#youtube).

## Common problems

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="YouTube requires a verified channel">
    You can verify your channel in YouTube Studio under **Settings**, **Channel**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="This channel hasn't been approved for long videos">
    Standard upload limits apply until YouTube approves your channel for long videos.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="YouTube's daily limit has been reached">
    Checks and uploads work again after midnight Pacific Time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="YouTube is restricting uploads on this channel">
    Check the channel's status in YouTube Studio.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
