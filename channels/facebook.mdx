OSCOHub publishes to Facebook **Pages**, not personal profiles. You need to manage the Page with your Facebook account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the connection">
    In the sidebar, select **+** next to **Social media**, then choose **Facebook**.

    <Screenshot name="connect-card-facebook" alt="The Connect a channel window with the Facebook card marked" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in to Facebook">
    Facebook opens in the same tab. Log in and accept every permission Facebook asks for. If you turn one off, some features won't work and you'll need to reconnect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your Pages">
    Back in OSCOHub, the **Select Facebook Pages** window lists every Page you manage. Select the ones you want, then select **Connect**. Each Page counts as one channel on your plan.

    <Screenshot name="connect-choose-pages" alt="The Select Facebook Pages window with one Page selected and the Connect button marked" caption="Example Pages. You'll see your own." />
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your Pages appear in the sidebar under **Social media**, with a green dot when they're ready to publish.

## What you can publish

Posts, reels and stories. See [formats and limits](/posts/formats-and-limits#facebook) for the details.

## Common problems

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My Page doesn't appear in the list">
    Check that your Facebook account manages the Page, and that you allowed access to it when Facebook asked. Then connect again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="OSCOHub says my Page is unpublished">
    Your audience won't see your posts until you publish the Page on Facebook.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="OSCOHub says the permission to publish is missing">
    A permission is missing. [Reconnect the channel](/channels/manage#reconnect-a-channel) and accept every permission Facebook asks for.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
