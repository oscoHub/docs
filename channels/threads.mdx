<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the connection">
    In the sidebar, select **+** next to **Social media**, then choose **Threads**.

    <Screenshot name="connect-card-threads" alt="The Connect a channel window with the Threads card marked" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in to Threads">
    Log in and accept every permission Threads asks for. Your profile then appears in the sidebar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can publish

Posts with text, images or one video, and threads made of several connected posts. Threads has no stories or reels. See [formats and limits](/posts/formats-and-limits#threads).
