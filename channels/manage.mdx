## Pause a channel

Select **Pause Page** to stop publishing to a channel. Select **Resume Page** to start again.

<Warning>
  A post that comes due while its channel is paused doesn't wait. It fails and moves to the **Issues** tab, so you'll need to [publish it again](/posts/manage-your-posts#retry-a-failed-post) after you resume. If you only want to delay a post, change its date instead.
</Warning>

## Check a channel

Select **Check & update** to test a channel. OSCOHub renews the login if it can, updates the channel's name and picture, and tells you about anything that would stop your posts going out.

OSCOHub also checks your channels by itself every day, and just before publishing to them.

## Reconnect a channel

Platform logins can expire, or stop working if you remove access or change your password. When that happens:

- The channel is marked **Needs attention** under the plug icon at the bottom of the sidebar.
- You get a notification.
- Posts to that channel fail until you reconnect it. Your scheduled posts are kept.

To fix it, select **Reconnect** and log in again. Accept every permission the platform asks for.

## Change the time zone

Each channel has its own time zone, which sets the time its posts go out. You can change it on the channel's page, above its posts.

<Screenshot name="channel-timezone" alt="The page of a channel with the time zone button above its posts marked" />

## Disconnect a channel

<Warning>
  Disconnecting removes the channel from OSCOHub. Any post still scheduled for that channel won't be published.
</Warning>

Select **Disconnect**, type the confirmation word, then confirm. This frees up a slot on your plan.

Disconnecting doesn't delete anything you've already published on the platform.

## When your plan no longer includes a platform

If you move to a plan without a platform, that channel stays connected and whatever is already scheduled still publishes. It can't take new posts until you upgrade.
