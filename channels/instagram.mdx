You need a **professional Instagram account (Business or Creator)**. You can connect it in either of two ways.

| | Connect Instagram | Connect Instagram with Facebook |
|---|---|---|
| **Best for** | Accounts with no Facebook Page | Accounts already linked to a Facebook Page |
| **You log in with** | Instagram | Facebook |
| **Facebook Page needed** | No | Yes |

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Connect Instagram">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Choose Instagram">
        In the sidebar, select **+** next to **Social media**, then choose **Instagram**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose the direct method">
        Select **Connect** under **Connect Instagram**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Log in">
        Log in to Instagram and accept every permission it asks for.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Connect Instagram with Facebook">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Choose Instagram">
        In the sidebar, select **+** next to **Social media**, then choose **Instagram**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose the Facebook method">
        Select **Connect** under **Connect Instagram with Facebook**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Log in to Facebook">
        Log in and accept every permission Facebook asks for.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose your accounts">
        Select the Instagram accounts to manage, then select **Connect**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## What you can publish

Posts, reels, stories and carousels. Every Instagram post needs an image or video, so text alone won't publish. See [formats and limits](/posts/formats-and-limits#instagram).

<Warning>
  Instagram doesn't let apps delete posts from accounts connected directly. To take down one of those posts, open it on Instagram and delete it there.
</Warning>

## Common problems

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="OSCOHub says the account is no longer linked to a Facebook Page">
    This applies when you connected through Facebook. Link the Instagram account to the Page again in Meta Business Settings, then [check the channel](/channels/manage#check-a-channel).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="OSCOHub says a permission is missing">
    [Reconnect the channel](/channels/manage#reconnect-a-channel) and accept every permission when asked.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
