Bluesky connects with an **app password**, a separate password you create just for OSCOHub. Don't enter your main Bluesky password.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an app password">
    In Bluesky, open [App Passwords](https://bsky.app/settings/app-passwords) and create a new one. Copy it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the connection">
    In OSCOHub, select **+** next to **Social media** in the sidebar, then choose **Bluesky**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your details">
    Enter your handle, such as `handle.bsky.social`, and paste the app password. Select **Connect**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can publish

Posts with text, up to 4 images or one video, and threads of up to 25 posts. See [formats and limits](/posts/formats-and-limits#bluesky).

## Common problems

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Connection failed">
    Check your handle, and make sure you pasted an app password rather than your main password.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Bluesky reports this account as deactivated or suspended">
    Posts can't be published until the account is reinstated.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
