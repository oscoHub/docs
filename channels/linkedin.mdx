OSCOHub publishes to your **personal LinkedIn profile**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the connection">
    In the sidebar, select **+** next to **Social media**, then choose **LinkedIn**.

    <Screenshot name="connect-card-linkedin" alt="The Connect a channel window with the LinkedIn card marked" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in to LinkedIn">
    Log in and accept the permissions LinkedIn asks for. Your profile then appears in the sidebar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you can publish

Text, images, one video, or a PDF document. See [formats and limits](/posts/formats-and-limits#linkedin).

## Common problems

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="OSCOHub says the permission to publish is missing">
    [Reconnect the channel](/channels/manage#reconnect-a-channel) and accept every permission LinkedIn asks for.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My statistics are missing">
    Your connection may not include permission to read statistics. See [Insights](/insights/overview#when-numbers-are-missing). Publishing isn't affected.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
