Telegram connects through a bot that you create and own. The bot must be an administrator of each group or channel you want to publish to.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your bot">
    In Telegram, search for **@BotFather** and send `/newbot`. Follow the prompts, then copy the **bot token** it gives you.

    <Warning>
      Your bot token is a secret, so never share it publicly.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make the bot an administrator">
    Open your group or channel settings and go to **Administrators**, then **Add Administrator**. Search for your bot by username. Give it permission to read and post messages, then confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a message">
    Send any message, such as "hello", in the group or channel so the bot can detect it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the connect window">
    In OSCOHub, select **+** next to **Messaging** in the sidebar, then choose **Telegram**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your token">
    Paste the bot token and select **Detect Groups & Channels**. Select the chats you want and select **Connect Telegram**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each group or channel you connect counts as one messaging channel on your plan.

## If your chat isn't detected

You can enter the chat ID yourself. Forward any message from your group to **@userinfobot** on Telegram to find it, enter it under **Or enter Chat ID manually**, then select **Verify Chat**.

## What you can publish

Text, a photo, a video, an album or a PDF document. You can also format your text and pin a message. See [formats and limits](/posts/formats-and-limits#telegram).

## Common problems

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The bot token is invalid">
    Check the token in BotFather and paste it again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Bot found, but not an administrator">
    Open the group's settings, go to **Administrators** and promote the bot. Then try again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The bot is missing the Post Messages permission">
    Open the channel settings, go to **Administrators**, edit the bot's permissions and turn on **Post Messages**. Then select **Fix & Try Again**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A webhook is already registered for this bot">
    The bot is already connected. Disconnect the existing connection in OSCOHub first, then try again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The bot can no longer reach this chat">
    Check that the bot is still a member and that the chat still exists.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
