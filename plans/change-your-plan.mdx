Select **Upgrade** in the top bar, or **Plan & billing** in your account pages, to see the plans.

## Upgrade

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Select **Upgrade** in the top bar to open the plans, then select **Choose** on the plan you want.

    <Screenshot name="plans-upgrade" alt="The plan cards for Free and Pro, with their price and what each includes" caption="Shown from a higher plan, so these buttons read Downgrade. Plans above yours read Choose." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the payment instructions">
    Payment is made by hand for now. OSCOHub shows you the bank transfer or PayPal details, or tells you they'll be sent by email. Include your email address in the payment reference, so we can match your payment to your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for activation">
    Your request stays pending until we confirm your payment, and a banner says it's being processed. Your new plan starts once it's activated, usually within 24 hours.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You keep your current plan and its limits while you wait.

<Note>
  OSCOHub doesn't take card payments yet, and it doesn't store payment card details.
</Note>

## Downgrade

Select **Downgrade** on a cheaper plan and confirm. When the change happens depends on your current plan:

| Your current plan | When the change happens |
|---|---|
| Has an end date | You keep it until that date, then it changes. You can select **Cancel Downgrade** until then. |
| Has no end date | It changes right away. |

If the plan you're moving to is a paid one, it starts once its payment is confirmed.

Once you've moved, the new plan's limits apply to new posts. Posts you've already scheduled still publish.

To stop paying, downgrade to Free.

## When your plan ends

If your paid plan ends without a renewal:

- Your posts, designs and settings all stay in your account.
- Paid features pause until you renew, and you'll see a prompt with a **Renew** button when you try to use one.
- Your scheduled posts are paused, and you get a notification.

When your plan is active again, paused posts whose time is still ahead go back on the schedule. Any whose time passed in the meantime become drafts, rather than going out late.
