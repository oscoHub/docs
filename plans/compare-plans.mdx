New accounts start on Free. The **Upgrade** page in OSCOHub always shows the latest prices and limits for your account.

| | Free | Pro | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Price per month** | $0 | $29 | $99 |
| **Social channels** | 3 | 5 | 10 |
| **Messaging channels** | 2 | 5 | Unlimited |
| **Connected stores** | 1 | 3 | Unlimited |
| **Posts per day** | 10 | 50 | Unlimited |
| **Posts per month** | 100 | 1,000 | Unlimited |
| **Uploads per day** | 100 MB | 5 GB | 20 GB |
| **Uploads per month** | 1 GB | 100 GB | 1 TB |
| **Designs** | 3 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **Post ideas** | 5 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **Recurring posts** | Included | Included | Included |
| **Store product import** | Included | Included | Included |
| **Product post kit** | Included | Included | Included |
| **Insights custom date range** | Not included | Included | Included |
| **Insights export** | Not included | Included | Included |

Prices exclude VAT and local taxes.

Every plan can connect all the [platforms available today](/channels/overview#supported-platforms) and [WooCommerce](/stores/connect-woocommerce).
