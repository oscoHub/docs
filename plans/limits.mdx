## Check your usage

Select the usage ring in the top bar. It shows your plan, your posts and uploads for today and this month, and how many channels and stores you've connected.

<Screenshot name="account-usage" alt="The usage panel open from the ring in the top bar, showing the plan and the posts, uploads, channels and stores used" />

The ring follows whichever limit is closest to full. It turns amber at 80% and red once you've reached a limit. Connection slots don't change its color, since using them all is normal.

## How posts are counted

- **Each channel counts as one post.** One post sent to three channels uses three.
- **A post counts when you create it,** on that day and in that month. That includes drafts and scheduled posts.
- **Deleting a post that hasn't gone out gives it back.**
- **A published post keeps counting,** even if you delete it afterwards.
- **Each repeat of a [recurring post](/posts/repeat-a-post) counts as a new post.**

The post window tells you how many posts you have left, and won't let you select more channels than that.

## What happens when you reach a limit

| Limit | What happens | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| **Posts per day** | You can't create more posts today. | Post again tomorrow, or upgrade. |
| **Posts per month** | You can't create more posts this month. | Wait for the date shown, or upgrade. |
| **Uploads per day** | You can't upload more today. | Upload again tomorrow, or upgrade. |
| **Uploads per month** | You can't upload more this month. | Wait for the date shown, or upgrade. |
| **Channels or stores** | You can't connect another. | Disconnect one, or upgrade. |
| **Designs or post ideas** | You can't save another. | Delete one, or upgrade. |

Posts you've already scheduled aren't affected, and still publish.

<Note>
  Designs you save count toward your upload limits, as well as your design limit.
</Note>

## Limits set by platforms

Platforms have their own limits, separate from your plan. See [platform daily limits](/posts/publish-and-schedule#platform-daily-limits) and [formats and limits](/posts/formats-and-limits).
