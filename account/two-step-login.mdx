With two-step login on, signing in also asks for a code from an authenticator app on your phone, such as Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Authy or Bitwarden.

## Turn it on

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your settings">
    Select the gear icon at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Profile settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the setup">
    Under **Two-step login**, select **Turn on** and confirm your password.

    <Screenshot name="account-two-step" alt="Profile settings with the Turn on button under Two-step login marked" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add OSCOHub to your app">
    Scan the QR code with your authenticator app. If you can't scan it, type the key into the app instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Enter the 6-digit code your app shows, then select **Turn on two-step login**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your recovery codes">
    Copy or download your recovery codes and keep them somewhere safe, such as a password manager.

    <Warning>
      You won't see these codes again. If you lose your phone, each code signs you in once.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sign in with a recovery code

On the two-step screen, select **Use a recovery code** and enter one of your saved codes. Each code works once. **Profile settings** shows how many you have left.

## Turn it off

Under **Two-step login**, select **Turn off** and confirm your password.

## If you lose your phone and your recovery codes

[Contact support](/help/contact-support). Our team can reset two-step login for you. You're then signed out everywhere and can set it up again at your next sign-in.
