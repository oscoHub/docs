## Change your language or theme

Select the gear icon at the bottom of the sidebar to open **Profile**, then look under **System preferences**.

- **Language:** English, Français or العربية. In Arabic, OSCOHub reads right to left.
- **Theme:** Light or Dark. You can also switch with the icon at the bottom of the sidebar.

## Your profile details

**Profile** shows your name, email, phone and time zone.

<Note>
  You can't edit your profile details yourself yet, so the **Save changes** button stays disabled. To change your email or another detail, [contact support](/help/contact-support).
</Note>

The **Email notifications** switches on this page aren't active yet either, so changing them has no effect. For now, OSCOHub tells you about your posts and channels through the bell in the top bar.

The time zone on your posts comes from the channel, and [you can change that](/channels/manage#change-the-time-zone).
