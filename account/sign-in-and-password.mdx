Sign in at [app.oscohub.com](https://app.oscohub.com/signin) with your email and password, Google or Facebook.

## Reset a forgotten password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request a code">
    On the sign-in page, enter your email to continue, then select **Forgot password?** Enter your email again and select **Send Code**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the code">
    Enter the 6-digit code from your email. If it doesn't arrive, check your spam folder, then select **Resend**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a new password">
    Enter a password of at least 8 characters, and repeat it. OSCOHub refuses passwords that are known to have leaked.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common problems

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="An account with this email already exists">
    This appears when you try Google or Facebook with an email that already has an account. Sign in with your password instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Too many attempts">
    Wait a few minutes and try again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The security check couldn't load">
    Check your connection or turn off any content blocker for the site, then try again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="This account has been deactivated">
    [Contact support](/help/contact-support) to restore it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Keep your account safe

Turn on [two-step login](/account/two-step-login), so a stolen password alone isn't enough to get in.
