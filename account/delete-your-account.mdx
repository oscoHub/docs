---
title: "Delete your account"
description: "Permanently delete your account and everything in it, with 14 days to change your mind."
---

<Warning>
  After 14 days, deletion is permanent and can't be recovered. [Download a copy of your data](/account/your-data) first if you want one.
</Warning>

## What you'll lose

- Every post, scheduled or published, and its media
- Your channel and store connections
- Your designs, ideas, templates and tags
- Your Community Hub ideas, comments and reviews. Ones other people replied to stay, shown as "Deleted user".
- Your plan. Paid subscriptions are not refunded.

Posts you've already published stay on the platforms. To remove them, delete them there first.

## Delete your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your settings">
    Select the gear icon at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Profile settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the deletion">
    Under **Delete account**, select **Delete my account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm it's you">
    Enter your password. If you sign in with Google or Facebook and have no password, we email you a 6-digit code instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the deletion">
    Type the word shown, then select **Delete my account**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## During the 14 days

Your scheduled posts are paused, and nothing publishes. You can still sign in, and a banner shows the date your account will be deleted.

## Change your mind

Sign in and select **Cancel deletion** on the banner. Your account comes back as it was, and your scheduled posts return to the schedule. Any post whose time passed while it was paused becomes a draft.
