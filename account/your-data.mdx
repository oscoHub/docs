## Download your data

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your settings">
    Select the gear icon at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Profile settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask for a copy">
    Under **Your data**, select **Prepare my data**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the email">
    We email you when your copy is ready. You can leave OSCOHub in the meantime.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download it">
    Open the link in the email, or return to **Your data**, and select **Download my data**. The download lasts 24 hours.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can ask for a new copy 24 hours after your last request. If preparing a copy fails, you can try again.

## What's in the download

A ZIP file of JSON files covering your profile, posts, channels, stores, designs, post ideas, post templates, tags, Community Hub activity, support requests, subscriptions, billing, notifications and usage.

## How long your media is kept

| Media | How long we keep it |
|---|---|
| Full-size images and videos on a published post | 90 days after publishing. A smaller preview and the link to the post stay. |
| Media on drafts and scheduled posts | Until the post publishes or you delete it. |

Deleting media from OSCOHub doesn't remove it from the platforms you published to.
