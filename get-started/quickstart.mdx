You need an account on at least one [supported platform](/channels/overview#supported-platforms), and permission to post to it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [app.oscohub.com](https://app.oscohub.com/signup) and sign up with your email, Google or Facebook. New accounts start on the Free plan. See [Create your account](/get-started/create-account) for each step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a channel">
    In the sidebar, select **+** next to **Social media**, then pick a platform and follow its steps.

    Each platform asks for something slightly different, so check its guide if you get stuck: [Facebook](/channels/facebook), [Instagram](/channels/instagram), [LinkedIn](/channels/linkedin), [Threads](/channels/threads), [YouTube](/channels/youtube), [Bluesky](/channels/bluesky) or [Telegram](/channels/telegram).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your post">
    Select **Create** at the top of the sidebar, then **Create Post**. Choose your channels and write your text. Add an image or video if the post type needs one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule it">
    Pick a date and time at the bottom of the window, then select **Schedule post**. To send it right away, open the arrow beside that button and choose **Publish now**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check that it went out">
    Open **Scheduling** in the sidebar. Your post sits on the calendar until its time comes, then moves to the **Published** tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>
