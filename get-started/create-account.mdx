## Sign up

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Email">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Enter your details">
        Go to the [sign-up page](https://app.oscohub.com/signup) and enter your full name and email.

        <Screenshot name="auth-signup" alt="The OSCOHub sign-up page with fields for name and email, and Google and Facebook buttons" />
      </Step>
      <Step title="Secure your account">
        Choose a password of at least 8 characters and add your phone number. OSCOHub refuses passwords that are known to have leaked.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Verify your email">
        Enter the 6-digit code we send to your email. If it doesn't arrive, check your spam folder, then select **Resend**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Google or Facebook">
    On the sign-up page, select **Google** or **Facebook** and approve the request.

    If Facebook doesn't share an email address with us, we ask you for one and send it a 6-digit code. The code works for 10 minutes.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
  If the security check at sign-up doesn't load, check your connection or turn off any content blocker for the site, then try again.
</Note>

## Finish setting up

The first time you sign in, OSCOHub asks a few questions:

| Question | Why we ask |
|---|---|
| **Phone number** | Required. It's saved on your account. |
| **Timezone** | Your posts follow this clock. We pick it from your browser, so change it if it's wrong. |
| **Language** | English, Français or العربية. You can [change it later](/account/preferences). |
| **Theme** | Light or dark. You can [change it later](/account/preferences). |
| **How did you find us?** | Required. It helps us understand how people discover OSCOHub. |

Next, you choose a plan. You can stay on Free and [upgrade](/plans/change-your-plan) whenever you need more.
