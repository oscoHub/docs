We reply within 24 hours on business days. Our answer goes to your account email.

## Send a request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Contact support page">
    Sign in, open your account menu and select **Contact us**, or go straight to [app.oscohub.com/contact](https://app.oscohub.com/contact).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a reason">
    Choose **Account & sign-in**, **Subscription & payments**, **Something is not working** or **Something else**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe what you need">
    Add a short subject and a message. Tell us what happened, what you expected, and the steps to see it again. Name the channel or store concerned, if any.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach a screenshot, if it helps">
    You can add one image, in PNG, JPG, GIF or WebP format, up to 10 MB. Leave out passwords and payment details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    Select **Send request**.

    <Screenshot name="help-contact-form" alt="The contact support form with a reason, your email, a subject, a message, an attachment area and the Send request button" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Follow up on a request

Your requests are listed on the same page under **Your requests**, each with its status.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Open** | We have it, and it's our turn to reply. |
| **Waiting for you** | We've replied and need something from you. |
| **Resolved** | We consider it done. Replying opens it again. |
