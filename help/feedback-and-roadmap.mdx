You'll find these pages under **Help & support** in your settings. Select the gear icon at the bottom of the sidebar to get there.

## Report a problem

Use this for bugs. Select **Report a Problem**, then give it a title, the affected platform, how serious it is, and a description of what you expected and what happened. Listing the exact steps helps us fix it quickly.

You can attach up to 5 files, as images, videos or PDFs, up to 20 MB each.

Your report gets a ticket number and its own page, where updates appear and you can talk to our team. You get a notification when the fix is live.

## Suggest a feature

Open **Feature ideas** and select **Submit Idea**.

<Screenshot name="help-community-ideas" alt="The Feature ideas page with sorting and status filters and the Submit Idea button" />
 Your title needs at least 10 characters and your description at least 20. You can attach one file up to 10 MB.

Our team reviews each idea before it appears publicly, and you get a notification with the decision. Once it's approved, others can vote on it.

On any idea, you can:

- **Vote** for it.
- **Follow** it, to get notified when its status changes.
- **Comment** on it. Comments appear once our team has reviewed them.

Sort by **Most Voted** or **Trending**, or by status, to see what's popular.

## See the roadmap

<Screenshot name="help-roadmap" alt="The roadmap page showing features by status" />

**Roadmap** shows what we're building, what's coming next and what's been released. Follow an item to be notified when its status changes or it gets an update.

## Leave a review

Open your account menu and select **Leave a review**. You can submit one review and edit it at any time. Our team reviews it before it appears, and editing sends it for review again.
