## A post failed to publish

Open **Scheduling**, go to the **Issues** tab, and read the reason on the post. You'll also find it in your notifications.

| The reason says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **The platform did not accept it** | The platform refused the post and gave the reason shown. | Fix what it describes, then [retry](/posts/manage-your-posts#retry-a-failed-post). Check [formats and limits](/posts/formats-and-limits) for media and text problems. |
| **Publishing took too long or was interrupted** | The post wasn't sent. | Publish it again. |
| **The channel was paused or disconnected** | The channel wasn't available when the post was due. | Resume or reconnect the channel, then reschedule the post. |
| **The store for this product is paused** | OSCOHub doesn't publish product posts for a paused store. | Resume the store, then publish the post again. |
| **The store for this product was disconnected** | The post can no longer be published. | Delete it. To post about the product again, reconnect the store and create a new post. |

Most other failures come from a channel whose login has expired, and [reconnecting the channel](/channels/manage#reconnect-a-channel) fixes them.

## A post didn't go out on time

Check these in order:

1. **Is it on the Pending tab?** If it's there, it may be waiting on a [platform's daily limit](/posts/publish-and-schedule#platform-daily-limits). Check your notifications.
2. **Is the time zone right?** Posts follow their channel's [time zone](/channels/manage#change-the-time-zone), which may differ from yours.
3. **Is your plan active?** Posts are [paused when a plan ends](/plans/change-your-plan#when-your-plan-ends).
4. **Is it about a store product?** A post about a scheduled product [waits for the product](/stores/post-about-a-product#what-happens-before-the-post-goes-out) to go live, for up to a day.
5. **Did you pick several channels?** One post sent to several channels goes out over about two minutes.

## A channel needs attention

Select **Check & update** on the channel to see what's wrong. The most common fix is to [reconnect it](/channels/manage#reconnect-a-channel) and accept every permission the platform asks for.

| OSCOHub says | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Your login expired, or was revoked** | Reconnect. This can happen if you removed access or changed your password. |
| **A permission is missing** | Reconnect and accept every permission. |
| **We could not find this account** | The account may have been deleted, or you may no longer manage it. |
| **The platform did not answer, or returned an error** | Try again in a few minutes. |
| **That's a lot of checks in a short time** | Wait a minute and try again. |

For problems specific to one platform, see its guide: [Facebook](/channels/facebook), [Instagram](/channels/instagram), [LinkedIn](/channels/linkedin), [Threads](/channels/threads), [YouTube](/channels/youtube), [Bluesky](/channels/bluesky) or [Telegram](/channels/telegram).

## I can't create a post or upload a file

You may have reached a limit on your plan. Check the usage ring in the top bar, then see [what happens when you reach a limit](/plans/limits#what-happens-when-you-reach-a-limit).

If your plan has ended, you'll be asked to [renew](/plans/change-your-plan#when-your-plan-ends).

## The Schedule post button is disabled

A channel's section isn't complete yet. Look for the **Incomplete** label in the post window. Each section tells you what it still needs, such as an image for an Instagram post.

## I can't see my statistics

See [when numbers are missing](/insights/overview#when-numbers-are-missing). New numbers can take up to 48 hours to appear.

## I can't sign in

See [sign in and reset your password](/account/sign-in-and-password). If you've lost your two-step login phone, see [two-step login](/account/two-step-login#if-you-lose-your-phone-and-your-recovery-codes).
