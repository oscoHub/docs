Insights shows statistics for the posts you publish **through OSCOHub**. Posts you publish directly on a platform aren't included.

## All your channels

Select **Insights** in the sidebar for the combined view:

- **Totals** for the period, compared with the period before. Only channels with numbers in both periods are compared.
- **Top posts**, which you can rank by the number you care about.
- **Channels**, a table of how each one did.
- **Followers** and **Posts published** charts. OSCOHub saves each channel's follower count once a day, so the chart fills in over time.

Filter by channel or by tag. Each channel's days follow its own time zone.

## One channel

Select **Insights** in a channel's menu, or select the channel in the table. You get a table of that channel's posts and their numbers. Use **Columns** to choose what's shown.


## One post

Select a post in a channel's table, or under **Top posts**, to see its statistics and when they were last refreshed.

## Choose a date range

Every plan can use **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days** and **Month to date**.

**Custom** ranges, up to one year long, and **Export CSV** depend on your plan. See [Compare plans](/plans/compare-plans).

## When numbers update

Platforms update their numbers slowly, so they can take up to 48 hours to appear.

OSCOHub collects a post's statistics 1, 2, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days after it's published. After that, opening the post asks for newer numbers if the ones it has are more than a day old.

## When numbers are missing

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Pending** | The numbers haven't arrived yet. | Check back later. |
| **Not available** | The platform has no number for this post. | Nothing. |
| **Reconnect to see statistics** | The channel's connection doesn't include permission to read statistics. Publishing isn't affected. | [Reconnect the channel](/channels/manage#reconnect-a-channel). |
| **Statistics aren't available yet** | The platform hasn't given OSCOHub access to these statistics yet. Publishing isn't affected. | Nothing. Reconnecting won't help. |
| **No statistics from this platform** | The platform doesn't share how individual posts performed. | Nothing. This applies to Telegram. |
